## Topics

- Aerodynamic lift

## Notes

- It explains how circulation around an airfoil can produce lift
- Kutta showed that the flow around a wing is not arbitrary; the flow at the trailing edge must leave smoothly
- This became known as the Kutta condition
- Combined with circulation theory, it provides a way to calculate the lift generated by an airfoil
- The work helped establish the theoretical foundation for later thin-airfoil theory
- Summary: Kutta’s key insight was that the way airflow leaves the trailing edge determines the circulation around the wing, and that circulation is directly related to lift.
