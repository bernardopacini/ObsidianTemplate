Lift is the aerodynamic force that acts perpendicular to the airflow around an object, such as an airplane wing.

## Basic Idea

A wing generates lift by changing the airflow around it. The pressure and velocity differences created around the wing result in an upward force.

## Lift Equation

$$L = 1/2 \rho v^2 S C_l$$
Where:

$L$ = Lift force
$\rho$ = Air density
$V$ = Airspeed
$S$ = Wing area
$C_l$ = Coefficient of lift


## Key Points

- Higher airspeed generally produces more lift.
- Larger wing area produces more lift.
- Higher air density produces more lift.
- $C_l$ depends on factors such as wing shape and angle of attack.
- If the angle of attack becomes too large, the wing can stall and lose lift.
